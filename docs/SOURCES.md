## What a source check establishes

Each listing records the original post, creator, discovery URL, check date and evidence level. The initial check uses X's official oEmbed endpoint for the primary post and FxTwitter for video attachment metadata and original-platform thumbnail URLs. A source check establishes that the post is accessible and carries video media; it does not independently reproduce the generation process or establish media licensing.

`primary-post` means the original post text explicitly names Fable 5.2. `upstream-index` means the discovery index attributes it to that model and primary confirmation is still needed. Detail pages show this distinction. Prompts are displayed only when recorded as publicly shared with a primary source. The initial selection quotes only short prompts; missing prompts remain missing.

Video titles are editorial descriptions, not creator quotations. Time-to-create claims in titles are creator/discovery claims, not measured benchmarks. The collection excludes unrelated interactive apps and static pages.

## Editorial choices

The homepage highlights six varied entries rather than loading every thumbnail. Full category pages and detail pages share a single JSON source across all five languages. Featured order is an editorial selection, not a quality ranking or a paid placement. No creator paid for inclusion in this initial collection.
