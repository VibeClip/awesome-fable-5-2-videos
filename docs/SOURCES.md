# Sources

Each entry links to the original creator and original publication. The catalog distinguishes a creator's model statement from an unconfirmed attribution. Post availability, media availability and an independently reproduced generation process are different checks.

The current catalog records public source-post checks, source media identifiers and dated engagement snapshots. Four older source posts were unavailable during this review; they remain listed with that status rather than being silently omitted from the coverage baseline. A video can also include other generation tools; such mixed workflows are recorded separately.

Titles are concise editorial descriptions. Time, cost and generation claims made by creators are not independent benchmarks. Public prompts remain in their original language and link to their original context. A missing prompt is never reconstructed or invented.

Short looping previews are stored in this repository. Full-video links point to the original creator's platform. There are no collection-site redirects, referral links or promotional tracking parameters in the browsing flow.

The machine-readable coverage baseline is in `data/reference.json`. New discoveries are distinguished from baseline coverage, and original media IDs are used to avoid counting reposts as new works.

Required notices for retained third-party material are centralized in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`. These notices are not promotional placements and do not grant rights to creator videos.
